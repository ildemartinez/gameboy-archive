## What counts as a limited edition?

The DMG-01 does not have one single, consistently documented catalog of limited editions. Collectors use the term for consoles produced or distributed in restricted circumstances, often identified by a special logo, branding, unusual shell, or a documented promotional release.

This category is separate from the standard color ranges covered in [Basic Colors](/consoles/dmg-01/physical-design/shell-housing/basic-colors/) and [Play It Loud! / Game Boy Bros.](/consoles/dmg-01/physical-design/shell-housing/play-it-loud/). A regular colored shell is not, by itself, evidence of a limited edition.

## Documented examples

Examples reported in collector references include:

| Example | Reported context | Identification notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red shell with Manchester United branding | UK market | A team-branded console; distinguish it from an unbranded red Play It Loud! unit. |
| Pink shell with Kirby branding | UK contest prize | A reported contest variant in a color outside the standard DMG-01 color ranges. |
| Off-white shell with Toyota branding | Japan | Reported as a promotional giveaway connected with Toyota car purchases. |
| Other logo-branded consoles | Various markets and promotions | Logos associated with organizations or events are recorded on otherwise familiar DMG-01 shells. |

These examples are not a complete checklist. Availability, production quantity, and provenance can be difficult to establish, and collector databases sometimes use “limited edition,” “special edition,” and “promotional” differently.

## Recording and verifying a variant

When documenting a claimed special edition, capture the front and rear of the console, close-ups of the logo and labels, the model and serial markings, and any original packaging or paperwork. Record the source of the claim and the market or promotion when known.

A logo or unusual shell alone does not establish that a console left the factory that way. Shells and labels can be replaced, and custom modifications exist. Treat attribution and production numbers as unverified unless supported by reliable contemporary material or clear provenance.
